## Cost-conscious discovery and verification

The executable workflow uses gpt-5.4-mini for two automated full-text review passes, classification and report drafting/checking, and the selected text-embedding-3-large for verified AI passages only. Every speech, including screened negatives, receives full-text review. Matching exact quotes and agreement are required for reviewed positives; disagreements remain Uncertain. Automated review is never described as human verification or inter-rater reliability. The report exports unresolved cases and coverage limits. Taxonomy is generated from actual discovery and source examples at execution time. Runtime context and API rates/caches are recorded; no paid inference has been used for implementation validation.

Local candidate screening is implemented in `unga_analysis/screening.py`, with a versioned lexicon in `config/ai_search_terms.json`. Run `python -m unga_analysis screen`, or `prepare` after adding a canonical transcript. The screen normalizes case, Unicode ligatures, hyphens and line breaks, searches across passage boundaries and retains original evidence locations. AI, artificial intelligence and superintelligence variants are searched alongside related concepts (for example machine learning and language models), context-dependent abbreviations (AGI/ASI/LLM) and separate indirect cues. These groups are search aids, not interchangeable definitions or reviewed labels. Review candidate context and screened negatives; a finite lexicon cannot guarantee every synonym, paraphrase or ASR error is caught. Outputs under `output/screening/` remain Pending and include input/lexicon/code hashes. Do not aggregate term hits or PDF comparison evidence as extra country-year observations.

A reference document's proposal is not evidence that a country endorsed it, and background notes do not enter country-level speech counts.

Track the Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Independent International Scientific Panel on AI, and proposed Global Fund for AI Capacity Development separately, verifying their status at the report cutoff. Distinguish an explicit name from an inferred reference, and code mention, welcome, support, concrete request, reservation/concern, opposition and commitment separately. Welcome acknowledges that a mechanism exists or was established. Support backs its work or continuation. Treat them as distinct stances. A commitment is an explicit pledge or undertaking by the speaker's State (such as contributing resources, hosting, or participating); record the actor, action and any specified timing/resources with evidence. Do not equate general support, an offer, a request directed at others, a future pledge and completed delivery. Multiple stance codes are allowed for the same country. Generic support for AI cooperation is not endorsement of a specific institution. Absence of a reference is not opposition. Recommendations should explicitly identify the relevant addressee: UN, SPMU, Tech Envoy, Panel and/or Dialogue.

For FSB, IAEA and other models, verify the actual proposer and record the requested functions (for example scientific assessment, coordination, monitoring, standard-setting or verification). Do not infer an inspection or enforcement mandate from a comparison alone. Keep institutional background sources separate from General Debate evidence and statistics.

Institutional stance records (institution_stances.csv): one row per country-year-mechanism-evidence passage, with these fields:
- year, iso3, speech_id, source file/hash, page or transcript locator, quote.
- mechanism: Dialogue, Panel, Fund, FSB-like, IAEA-like, IPCC-like, CERN-like, new body/verification regime, other named model.
- reference_type: explicit name, name variant (for example "Global Fund for AI", "AI capacity-building fund") or inferred description (for example "a UN body to verify AI systems").
- stance flags (0/1, several may be 1): mention, welcome, support, request, concern, opposition, commitment.
- requested_functions: a multi-select from a fixed vocabulary of dialogue/convening, scientific assessment, capacity building, financing, norms/rules/standards, monitoring/oversight/verification, coordination, other (describe). Use an empty value when no function is stated.
- un_role: explicit, meaning the UN or a named UN mechanism is asked to do something, or generic, meaning a call for international cooperation that names no UN role. Only explicit rows count as requests to the UN.
- commitment details (actor, action, timing/resources) when commitment = 1.
- review_status and rationale.

Role of UN reports two tables built from reviewed rows. The first counts countries by mechanism and stance, and shows n and the country list. The second lists the main models with their proposer, requested functions and a short evidence quote. A country counts once per mechanism-stance cell in each year.
